## Paper 1
- Citation: S. Haque, M. Keffeler, and T. Atkison, "An evolutionary approach of attack graphs and attack trees: A survey of attack modeling," in Proc. Int. Conf. Security and Management (SAM'17), CSREA Press, 2017, pp. 224-229.
- What it does (1 line): A survey of attack modelling with attack graphs and attack trees, covering alert correlation, graph generation, tree reduction and Bayesian risk analysis, with the limits of each.
- Difference from AttackPath (1 line): It reviews methods and builds no tool; AttackPath ranks paths from a plain Nmap scan and is evaluated against ground truth.
- Points to use: nodes are states and edges are transitions; large graphs suffer state explosion; logic-based generation depends on admin-defined rules; one cited work uses CVSS in a Bayesian attack graph.
